How LifeLink works

A simple process to help people coordinate blood donation.

♙

1. Register

Donors can create a profile, choose their blood group and share a broad location.

⌕

2. Find support

Requesters can search consenting donors by blood group and area.

♡

3. Coordinate

Create a request, track its status and coordinate with the relevant organization.

Important: This website supports donor discovery and coordination only. It does not determine medical eligibility or blood compatibility. Final screening and transfusion decisions must be made by qualified healthcare professionals.

Your privacy matters

The SRS requires donor contact information to be shared only with appropriate consent. This demo displays only a donor's name, blood group and broad area. It does not publish email addresses or phone numbers.

This is a front-end demonstration. It does not verify identity, medical eligibility or donor availability.
